## Why dual

Code wants permissive reuse with patent-grant clarity (Apache) or maximum
permissiveness (MIT). Predictions and framework documentation are an
epistemic track record — they should be citable, attributable, and not easily laundered into someone else's "proprietary intelligence product." CC BY 4.0 is the cleanest fit for that.
